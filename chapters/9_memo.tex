\noindent \textbf{To:} Show Management \\
\textbf{Subject:} Recommendation for a More Professional Yet Watchable Voting System

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent \textbf{Executive message.}
Controversy concentrates in weeks where judges' evaluations and audience preferences diverge.
To prioritize technical merit while preserving audience agency, we recommend piloting
\textbf{Professional Bottom-2 (PB2)}, a judges-gated two-stage policy, validated by replay-based stress tests
(Jaccard agreement, controversial-case replays, and a unified bias test). \cite{comap2026_problemC_pdf}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent \textbf{Baseline (current mechanism).}
The incumbent format is effectively \emph{combined Bottom-2 + judges' save}:
contestants are ranked by a combined judge--fan score; the Bottom-2 form an at-risk set; judges choose who leaves. \cite{comap2026_problemC_pdf}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent \textbf{Recommendation (PB2).}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Judges-only gate (risk set).} Rank contestants by judges' performance share $q^{J}_{i,t}$ and designate Bottom-2
  (optionally Bottom-$k$ late in the season) as the at-risk set.
  \item \textbf{Fan-aware decision within the gate.} Eliminate the contestant in the gate with the lower blended score
  \[
    S_{i,t}= w_J\, q^{J}_{i,t} + (1-w_J)\, p_{i,t},
  \]
  where $p_{i,t}$ is inferred/proxy fan vote share. \cite{comap2026_problemC_pdf}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent \textbf{Why PB2 improves professionalism.}
Under PB2, elimination is always decided among judges-defined low performers, so extreme popularity cannot
keep a weak performer out of the risk set; professionalism becomes a structural property, not a side effect. \cite{comap2026_problemC_pdf}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent \textbf{How to preserve watchability (no randomness).}
Use a phase-dependent schedule: early weeks keep Bottom-2 gating; late in the season (few contestants, narrow gaps),
enable structured suspense by expanding the gate to Bottom-$k$ and/or placing a small number of structured multi-elimination weeks,
with eliminations restricted to the judges-defined low-performance band. \cite{comap2026_problemC_pdf}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent \textbf{Management knobs and deployment.}
PB2 offers interpretable controls: (i) $w_J$ (judge--fan balance within the gate),
(ii) a late-stage trigger (e.g., remaining contestants $\le K$ or week threshold $t_0$),
and (iii) gate size $k$ (plus optional multi-elimination placement).
We recommend selecting a robust operating point supported by stress tests and running PB2 in \emph{one-season shadow mode}
computed in parallel with the broadcast rule. \cite{comap2026_problemC_pdf}
