\subsection{Design Goals and Principles}
\label{subsec:d2_design_goals}

Our replay analysis shows that outcome sensitivity concentrates in weeks where judges' evaluations and
audience preferences diverge, and that purely rank-based aggregation can amplify small ordering
differences into large outcome changes. A redesigned system should therefore:
(i) preserve audience agency while (ii) preventing extreme popularity from overwhelming technical performance,
(iii) remain interpretable to viewers, (iv) use few tunable parameters, and (v) be robust to vote-estimation
uncertainty and minor format variations. We operationalize these goals with measurable criteria:
high replay fidelity on eligible weeks (KPI1), reduced instability under perturbations (stability under uncertainty),
and transparent parameterization for management control.

\subsection{Proposed aggregation rule: Judge Safeguard + Smoothed Rank (JS-SR)}
\label{subsec:d2_js_sr}

We propose an implementable rule that balances technical merit and audience agency by introducing a
\emph{Safeguard Zone}. Pure percent blending can allow extreme popularity to dominate, while pure rank aggregation
can discard meaningful score margins. JS-SR protects top technical performers while letting fan preference decide outcomes
within the remaining ``battleground'' set.

\paragraph{Step 1: Safeguard threshold.}
For each week $t$ with active set $A_{s,t}$, let $\tau\in(0,1)$ be a management-controlled percentile (e.g., $\tau=0.30$
meaning ``top 30\% by judges'').
Define the week-specific threshold on raw total judges' scores $J_{i,t}$:
\begin{equation}
\text{Threshold}_{s,t} = \text{Percentile}\big(\{J_{i,t}\}_{i\in A_{s,t}},\, 1-\tau\big).
\end{equation}
The safeguarded set is
\begin{equation}
\text{SafeSet}_{s,t}=\{i\in A_{s,t}\mid J_{i,t}\ge \text{Threshold}_{s,t}\}.
\end{equation}
Contestants in $\text{SafeSet}_{s,t}$ are immune from elimination in week $t$.

\paragraph{Step 2: Battleground scoring and elimination.}
Let $D_{s,t}=A_{s,t}\setminus \text{SafeSet}_{s,t}$ be the battleground set.
For $i\in D_{s,t}$, define the combined score using judges' share $q^J_{i,t}$ and inferred fan share $p_{i,t}$:
\begin{equation}
S^{(JS)}_{i,t}=w_J\, q^J_{i,t} + (1-w_J)\, p_{i,t}, \qquad w_J\in[0,1].
\end{equation}
We eliminate the $m_{s,t}$ contestants with the smallest $S^{(JS)}_{i,t}$ among $D_{s,t}$ (deterministic tie-handling).


\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{Algorithm 3: JS-SR replay under $(\tau, w_J)$}
\label{alg:js_sr_replay}
\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]  % [1] 显示行号，algpseudocode 必须保留
    % ---- 输入定义（首字母大写，参数分行排版避免溢出）----
    \Require 
        Weekly active sets $A_{s,t}$, raw judges totals $J_{i,t}$, judges shares $q^J_{i,t}$, inferred fan shares $p_{i,t}$,
        elimination counts $m_{s,t}$, parameters $(\tau, w_J)$.
    % ---- 输出定义（首字母大写）----
    \Ensure 
        Eliminated sets $\tilde E^{(JS)}_{s,t}$ and final placements under JS-SR.

    % ---- 核心算法逻辑（适配 algpseudocode 关键字）----
    \For{$t=1$ to $T_s$}
        \State Restrict to active set $A_{s,t}$.
        \If{$m_{s,t}=0$}
            \State $\tilde E^{(JS)}_{s,t}\leftarrow \varnothing$; continue to next week.
        \EndIf
        % 计算阈值（优化变量排版，用 \mathrm 统一格式）
        \State Compute $\mathrm{Threshold}_{s,t}$ as the $(1-\tau)$ percentile of $\{J_{i,t}\}_{i\in A_{s,t}}$.
        % 定义安全集和待淘汰池（拆分行提升可读性）
        \State Form safe set: 
        \[
        \mathrm{SafeSet}_{s,t} = \{i\in A_{s,t}: J_{i,t}\ge \mathrm{Threshold}_{s,t}\}
        \]
        \State Form elimination candidate pool: $D_{s,t} = A_{s,t}\setminus \mathrm{SafeSet}_{s,t}$.
        \State For each $i\in D_{s,t}$, compute combined score:
        \[
        S^{(JS)}_{i,t} = w_J q^J_{i,t} + (1-w_J)p_{i,t}
        \]
        \State Eliminate the $m_{s,t}$ contestants with smallest $S^{(JS)}_{i,t}$ within $D_{s,t}$ (deterministic tie-handling applied).
        \State Update next week's active set: $A_{s,t+1}\leftarrow A_{s,t}\setminus \tilde E^{(JS)}_{s,t}$.
    \EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}



\subsection{Stress Testing and Simulation Evidence via Replay}
\label{subsec:d2_stress_testing}

We evaluate the proposed rule using a season--week replay protocol identical to Chapters~4--5, ensuring fair
comparisons across rule variants. Stress tests sweep $(\tau ,w_J)$ over a predefined grid and propagate vote
uncertainty by sampling plausible vote scenarios within Task~A uncertainty bands (or bootstrap reconstructions).
For each configuration, we report KPI1 on eligible weeks, divergence relative to historical rules, and stability
metrics quantifying how often small parameter or vote perturbations change (i) the eliminated set and (ii) final placements.
Recommended parameters are selected by multi-metric trade-off analysis, prioritizing robust performance rather than tuning
to any single season.
